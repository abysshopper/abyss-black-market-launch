### Four different meanings of “supported”

1. **Implementation capability:** a codec, token/reward implementation, hook base or constructor exists. It cannot authorize a launch.
2. **Frozen known preset:** release-owned construction metadata matches a known chain/core/profile. `supported` and stored `enabled` describe that snapshot; presets deliberately contain no current `admitted` certificate.
3. **Example/qualification:** a fixed runner demonstrates particular inputs, or a hook has fork-local behavioral evidence. Neither is certification of every combination or production registry admission.
4. **Live-admitted offering and plan:** fresh pinned discovery certifies the selected profile, then current exact-plan simulation proves its requested token, markets, economics, funding, buys and execution mode. Even `profile.admitted` alone does not certify that combination.

### Fee schedule and accounting are separate axes

- **Trading-rate units:** 1,000,000 pips = 100%; 10,000 pips = 1%; 3,000 pips = 0.30%. Config6 requires integer `0 <= minimumHookFeePips <= hookFeePips <= 1,000,000` and sensitivity **0..4,294,967,295**, in **fee-pips × seconds/tick**. LP fee must be below 1,000,000 pips, with concrete-hook restrictions additionally enforced. [N-guide, N-markets]
- **Fixed schedule:** applicable hook charge floors `amount × hookFeePips / 1,000,000`; the stored minimum/sensitivity do not make it dynamic. Example maximum/minimum/sensitivity **10,000 / 1,000 / 7,654,321** are example economics, not recommended user defaults. [N-guide, N-example]
- **Dynamic schedule:** rate is `min(maximum, minimum + floor(positiveObservedTickChange × sensitivity / ageAdjustedElapsedSeconds))`; missing signal/elapsed time or flat/falling movement uses minimum. Zero sensitivity fixes the rate at minimum; equal bounds fix the rate; maximum zero requires minimum zero and gives free hook trading. The rate freezes before observers, so a swap cannot raise its own charge. Clamp lag means fees may remain elevated while raw spot is flat; idle time reduces the old signal. This is not a trade-size tax and not a manipulation-resistance guarantee. Rate preview is not a guaranteed future execution quote. [N-guide, H-guide, H-dynamic]
- **Hook fee currency:** `InputToken` (mode **0**, flag **1**) collects the trader's input asset; `QuoteOnly` (mode **1**, flag **2**) collects the designated quote asset in either direction. Flag **3** permits either mode, not both assets on one swap. Address ordering does not select the quote. [H-guide; N-markets]
- **LP versus hook fees:** LP fees and hook-delta fees are distinct sources. A quote-only **hook** does not make every fee in a fixed pool quote-only when nonzero LP fees can also accrue. Inspect the actual collector and all potentially collected assets, rather than derive the reward-asset set from hook mode alone. Zero-LP authoring/dynamic offerings generate hook-fee proceeds, not LP fees. [N-example, H-guide]
- **Protocol and disposition units:** protocol treasury denominator, executor bounty, owner/rewards/burn disposition, HMM strategy share and author royalty are not trading pips. Current frozen fixed/dynamic/HMM presets use protocol denominator **6**, author maximum **0 bps**, and record a protocol author ceiling **2,400 bps**; that larger ceiling does not allow exceeding an offering's zero maximum. Read these live/frozen terms, do not hardcode them into a plan. [N-presets]
- **Hub economics:** each actual asset received by the hub first pays the executor bounty on newly collected proceeds; the remainder follows the committed owner/rewards/burn split totaling **10,000 bps**. Author allocation is then floor-rounded from each source's contribution to owner proceeds, not from rewards/burn and not as an extra swap surcharge. A 500-bps author declaration means 5% of the applicable owner proceeds, not a 5% trade tax. Stable author identity is not the mutable live payout address. **HMM diversion happens upstream of the hub**: protocol treasury sweeps first, then the collector's chosen share of newly collected net quote funds the manager, then bounty/disposition/author accounting applies to the forwarded remainder. Token-side fees are not diverted. 100% share eliminates this source's net quote hub receipts, not its protocol payment; zero share does not disable an already-funded manager. [N-guide, H-guide, HMM-protocol; details in [HMM](hmm.md)]
- Hook treasury accounting in the authoring base is separately rounded: zero denominator means no treasury amount; otherwise it applies division by the denominator, then the documented 125/100 adjustment. Nonzero authoring denominators are 4..10. Do not flatten this into an assumed percentage of the user's input or reuse it as price math. Authoring hooks also reject a nonzero PoolManager protocol fee instead of silently adding another charge. [H-guide]

### Oracle and topology restrictions

Current fixed/dynamic/**HMM Fixed/HMM Dynamic** frozen envelopes allow spacing **1..32,767**, at most **32 positions**, oracle cardinality at most **4,096**, and both hook fee modes. These are per-profile bounds, not a guarantee that every setting fits execution or that a hook composes an oracle. HMM Fixed remains oracle-free notwithstanding these wire-field bounds. Global launch limits still apply. [N-presets, HMM-fixed-review]

The oracle config is creator-selected, not a profile-pinned ID. V4 validation reads the certified factory mapping and requires a positive maximum tick move no greater than **887,272**, cardinality at least **2**, and cardinality no greater than the selected envelope maximum. The documented Robinhood P1/P2/P3 settings have maximum moves **1/6/17** and cardinality **4,096**; their presence must be checked at the pinned block. Do not invent an oracle ID for a quote. [N-discovery, N-guide, P-guide]

For authoring hooks, the core-only base returns oracle genesis zero; constructor fields and registered config alone do not create oracle history. The optional truncated-oracle template provides genuine genesis, clamped quote-normalized sampling, retained-history queries and capacity growth. Capacity is not fabricated observations or mature lookback; oracle existence is not lending-feed maturity/depth/recency certification. This workflow requires actual oracle-enabled behavior, not merely an oracle field. [H-guide, N-guide]

V4 creators choose `externalLiquidityDisabled` per pool. Allowing external liquidity permits third-party add/remove after opening, without releasing permanent launch custody or pre-opening protection. Bound hook parameters, fees, terms and full-key/economic commitment must freeze before salt mining. Hook permission bits alone, successful CREATE2 mining, codec output or an unsigned builder are never admission. [N-guide, P-guide]

- Atomic/staged is explicit and does not change eligibility. Staged preparation cannot split mint/lock/all ordered buys/public activation. Token-transfer restrictions plus canonical opening checks remain required on both venues; do not substitute a pool-gate assumption.
- HMM config6 requires the selected profile's canonical V6 prefix and **exactly 448 canonical tail bytes**; no missing/extra tail, tail on ordinary profiles, changed dynamic offsets or historical config version. Validate all strategy ABI widths/ranges/caps and **zero LP/zero author** economics; preserve the tail through decode, salts, plan serialization/recovery and exact proof. ## Exact decision procedure for a requested combination

A requested combination is valid for this workflow only when **all** these gates pass. Preserve the distinction between profile eligibility and executable exact-plan admission.

1. **Resolve the actual domain.** Read installed SDK exports/release and actual chain/core/registry/deployment helpers; confirm RPC chain identity and registry-to-core binding. Unknown preset identity is not permission to borrow the known graph: explicitly discover that deployment. Retain source/version and pinned canonical block number/hash.
2. **Discover the requested profile, not a convenient substitute.** Use Node `readLifecycleProfiles({client, orchestrator, profileIds? / offset / limit})` or Python `read_lifecycle_profiles(client, orchestrator=..., offset=..., limit=...)`. Page through the actual registry when enumerating; a partial first page is not a complete catalog. Underlying getters are `profileCount`, `profileIds`, `adapterCount`, `adapterIds`, `profile`, `adapter`, `profileTopology`, and `requireEligible`. Keep unavailable reads, disabled/retired rows, `admitted: false` and their original `reason` visible.
3. **Require a genuine certificate.** The selected live row must be `admitted`, with the requested adapter/profile, enabled registration/adapter, supported schema and exact topology/config version. `requireEligible` must resolve the expected adapter for the version/required capabilities. Abyss must be exact canonical chain/factory/variant/schema with no V4 hook graph. V4 additionally needs the reviewed envelope and matching enabled developer terms/current protocol ceiling. Reviewed artifact/review/terms/bounds, capabilities, runtime/dependency graph, manager/oracle/locker/collector/deployer and constructor/creation provenance must certify. Shared roots and bound instances require their respective exact constructor/runtime checks; permission flags alone are insufficient. [N-discovery, N-guide]
4. **Check requested token/reward and accounting, not only family name.** Require needed ERC404/multi-position capabilities and actual token implementation restrictions. Bind the actual potential collected assets, reward support, owner/rewards/burn destinations, disposition totals and funding. Standard/Quote and InputToken/QuoteOnly are explicit user economic choices. Do not rewrite a requested mode, discard an asset, invent token burn from quote proceeds, or remove rewards after refusal.
5. **Bind exact market economics and oracle policy.** Schema/inner/outer versions and profile IDs must agree. V4 config binds that profile's stable beneficiary, terms digest, treasury, denominator, selected author rate within both reviewed and live ceilings, supported mode, bounds and concrete-hook constraints (including ordinary dynamic and both HMM offerings' zero LP/zero author). For HMM, validate and retain the exact strategy tail, quote diversion share/fee precedence, thresholds/caps/premium/decay and mutable fee-owner authority as reviewed economics; a default helper is not approval. Verify registered fee/spacing and oracle mapping at the pinned block. Require genuine oracle-enabled implementation behavior; **refuse HMM Fixed** and other non-oracle offerings even if their registry row is admitted and schema/config parses. Enforce full-key binding, quote orientation, custody, allocations, topology and duplicate-quote constraints.
6. **Freeze and prove the full requested plan.** Use Node `prepareAndPlanLifecycleLaunch` / Python `prepare_and_plan_lifecycle_launch` with explicit mode and protected ordered buys, or the appropriate finalized-plan admission API. Require actual `admitted`, proved execution and applicable protocol fit for the exact commitment, all required funding/conversions and indivisible activation. Record transport preflight separately; it is not sequential proof. Unavailable/refused simulation stops. Robinhood needs its real Nitro evidence/exact sequential backend, not a generic-fork substitute. Fresh next-envelope proof is required before user-authorized execution. [N-guide, P-guide]

**Retain refusal evidence:** store the selected domain/profile/config, observed block, SDK `reason`, error code/message and available native revert/cause. For example, unsupported schema, uncertified topology, missing capabilities, profile/dependency/terms mismatch, author ceiling, unregistered oracle, out-of-bounds positions, incompatible reward assets, duplicate V4 quote, missing tail, funding failure and execution-capacity refusal are different outcomes. A read/transport failure is “unavailable,” not fabricated profile rejection or success. Do not squash all failures into “unsupported hook,” silently fall back from oracle to non-oracle, fixed to dynamic, config6 to historical config5, atomic to staged, or remove markets/buys/protection to get a passing result.

## Registration is not arbitrary customization

Supported launch customization means composing certified public plans with user-selected admitted economics. It does **not** mean entering an arbitrary hook address, adding taxes/blacklists/minting, patching Solidity, inventing oracle data or registering a template on the user's behalf.

Abyss canonical registration is an admin-certified factory/variant route. V4 production admission requires maintainer review of the complete artifact and economic terms, deployed dependency graph, exact reviewed envelope/profile and registry author-controller authorization/admin submission. Hook submission support in Abyss Hooks is **PoolBoundV4 only**; local structure checks, inherited-base compatibility, fork tests, provenance, name/slug or the 500-bps reference declaration do not grant that production admission. Authoring hooks enforce their exact declared hub author allocation before acquiring liquidity; a generic envelope ceiling is not permission to ignore concrete-hook enforcement. [P-guide, H-guide]

Profile retirement prevents new/pending source binding but does not rewrite frozen active-source economics or disable stable-author claims. Existing historical offerings can be documented and reconciled without making them defaults for a new launch. Finish selection only with current evidence for the user's exact combination; this catalog never supplies a live certification on its own.
